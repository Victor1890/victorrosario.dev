# gob.do – Dominican Republic Government Services Portal

The official unified government services portal for the Dominican Republic — providing citizens with access to passport renewal, social security, travel permits for minors, and hundreds of other government services. Built as part of the Agenda Digital initiative by OGTIC and Burocracia Cero.

## Overview

### The Challenge

As a high-traffic government platform serving millions of Dominican citizens, gob.do required constant maintenance, security hardening, and issue resolution. The portal integrates multiple government institutions and services, making reliability and security critical — any vulnerability or downtime directly impacts citizen access to essential services.

## My Contributions

### What I Worked On

As a **developer**, I provided ongoing support and improvements across the full stack:

- **Vulnerability remediation** — identified and resolved security vulnerabilities across the frontend and backend, ensuring the platform met government security standards for handling citizen data.
- **Issue resolution** — debugged and fixed production issues affecting user experience, from UI glitches to backend API failures.
- **Support & maintenance** — provided continuous support for the platform, ensuring uptime and reliability for a service that handles passport renewals, social security queries, and travel permits for thousands of daily users.

### What I Learned

Working on gob.do taught me the importance of **security in government applications** and the unique challenges of maintaining a **high-traffic public service platform**. I learned how to prioritize **vulnerability fixes** without disrupting service, and the value of **proactive support** in keeping critical infrastructure running for millions of citizens.
